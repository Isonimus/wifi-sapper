WiFi Sapper is a security research tool for **authorized** industrial and wireless penetration
testing, education, and defensive research. It captures WPA/WPA2 handshakes and — only when the
operator explicitly arms it — transmits deauthentication frames to force a handshake. It is
intended for security professionals testing networks they own or are contracted to assess.
